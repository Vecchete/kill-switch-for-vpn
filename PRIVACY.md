# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Kill Switch for VPN doesn't collect, store or send any personal data, and it doesn't contact any server.

Everything it handles stays in your browser:

- **The proxy setting.** The extension reads it to know whether a VPN extension is connected. It never changes it.
- **The IDs of tabs that had a page load blocked.** They are kept in memory for the current browser session, so those tabs can be reloaded once the VPN connects. The addresses of those pages are not kept.
- **Two settings:** whether the kill switch is on, and whether the local network is allowed while the VPN is off. They are stored in the browser's extension storage on your device.

Nothing is shared with the developer or with anyone else.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Vecchete/kill-switch-for-vpn/issues.
